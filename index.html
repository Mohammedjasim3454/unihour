UniHour Platform Overview

UniHour is a smart platform designed to connect students with local businesses that require short-term hourly workers. Through a simple and user-friendly interface, students can browse nearby job opportunities, apply for flexible shifts, and earn extra income while continuing their studies. At the same time, employers can quickly post temporary job requirements and hire verified students for tasks such as event support, delivery assistance, or retail help. The platform offers key features like location-based job matching, real-time job postings, secure student verification, and a transparent system that builds trust between employers and students. By combining these features, UniHour creates a beneficial ecosystem where students gain financial independence and practical experience while businesses receive reliable and timely workforce support.
